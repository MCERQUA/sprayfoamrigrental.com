Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifying the grey areas of rig acquisition.

How do rental terms handle maintenance breakdowns? expand_more
Most reputable rental agreements cover standard mechanical failures through a replacement or field-service model. However, user-error damages (like cured foam in lines due to improper flushing) fall squarely on the renter. Always review the "wear and tear" clauses.
Are there insurance gaps when renting vs owning? expand_more
Yes. Owned equipment is typically scheduled on an Inland Marine policy. Rented equipment requires "Leased or Rented Equipment" coverage. Limits must match the full replacement value of the rig, not just the rental contract value.
Who handles preventative maintenance? expand_more
For owned equipment, you are responsible for all PM schedules (generator oil changes, proportioner servicing). For rentals, daily PMs (greasing, flushing) are the renter's job, while major scheduled service is handled by the rental house between deployments.
What are the tax implications (Section 179)? expand_more
Purchased rigs often qualify for Section 179 deduction, allowing you to deduct the full purchase price in the year acquired. Rental payments are generally written off as standard operating expenses. Consult your CPA for specifics to your state and corporate structure.
