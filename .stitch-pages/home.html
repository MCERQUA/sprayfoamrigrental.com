Why Rig Rental Is a Real Decision

trending_up

Seasonal Capacity Management

Taking on large commercial projects during peak season often requires more rigs than you currently own. Renting bridges the gap without permanent fleet expansion.

settings_backup_restore

Backup Equipment

When your primary proportioner goes down for unexpected maintenance, a short-term rental keeps your crew working and prevents costly project delays.

science

Testing New Service Lines

Before committing $80,000+ to a dedicated polyurea coating rig, renting allows you to test market demand and train your crew with minimal risk.

account_balance

Avoiding Capital Outlay

Preserve cash flow for payroll and materials. Rental costs can often be passed directly through to specific project budgets rather than sitting on your balance sheet.

Equipment Primer

Understanding the core components you are actually renting.

opacity

Proportioner

The heart of the rig. It heats and pressurizes the A and B chemicals to exact ratios. Rental units are typically hydraulic or pneumatic.

water_drop

Spray Gun

Where the chemicals mix. High-wear item requiring rigorous cleaning. Often rented with strict maintenance penalties.

route

Heated Hose

Maintains chemical temperature from proportioner to gun. Usually rented in 50ft sections up to 200ft total length.

bolt

Generator

Heavy-duty power supply (often 40kw+) required to run the heaters, compressor, and proportioner simultaneously in remote sites.
