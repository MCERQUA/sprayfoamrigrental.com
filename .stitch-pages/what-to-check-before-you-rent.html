What to Check Before You Rent a Rig

A neutral, uncompromising checklist for evaluating any rental provider. Ensure your project stays on track by verifying the critical details before you sign a temporary agreement.

shield

Insurance and Liability

assignment_turned_in Provider Requirements

Request explicit confirmation of what policies the provider carries. Standard commercial auto is insufficient for specialized equipment transport.

  • check_circle Inland Marine: Covers the rig and equipment while in transit to your site.
  • check_circle General Liability: Covers bodily injury and property damage related to the equipment operation.

assignment_ind Renter Requirements

Understand what documentation you must provide before handover.

  • document_scanner Certificate of Insurance (COI): Usually required listing the provider as additionally insured.
  • local_shipping Towing Endorsements: If you are transporting the rig yourself.
route

Delivery and Logistics

map

Service Area

Confirm exact mileage limits. Rentals outside primary service areas often incur steep per-mile surcharges or require multi-day minimums.

payments

Delivery Fees

Ask for an itemized quote. Drop-off and pick-up fees are rarely included in the base daily/weekly rental rate.

straighten

Site Clearance

Verify required clearance dimensions, terrain stability (gravel vs. mud), and shore power requirements before scheduling.

gavel

Rental Terms

Term Variable What to Look For
calendar_clock Minimum Periods Are you locked into a full week if you only need 3 days? Check for weekend grace periods.
account_balance_wallet Deposit Policies Determine if the deposit is a hold or an actual charge, and document the specific damage assessment criteria for return.
cancel Cancellation Terms Weather delays happen. Ensure you understand the penalty tiers for canceling 24, 48, or 72 hours out.
settings_applications

Compatibility & Support

science

Chemical Calibration

Spray foam rigs must be calibrated specifically for the chemistry you intend to spray. Ensure the A/B ratio pumps are set correctly for your chosen material brand before the rig leaves the lot.

support_agent

Technical Lifeline

Equipment jams cost time. Clarify if the provider offers on-call technical support during weekend or evening hours, and if basic operation training is included in the handover.

request_quote

Pricing Structure Analysis

To achieve an apples-to-apples comparison across providers, mandate quotes that break down the base structure:

Per-Unit Rates
Daily base rate, standard for short-term fixes.
Tiered Pricing
Discounts applied at day 3, 7, or 14.
Flat Time-Blocks
Fixed fee for a set window (e.g., weekend warrior package).
warning

warning Provider Red Flags

Walk away if a rental provider exhibits any of these behaviors:

  • close
    No Insurance Proof Offered If they cannot provide an active COI immediately upon request.
  • close
    Vague Agreement Terms No formal contract outlining liability, start/end times, and damage thresholds.
  • close
    Unclear Damage Liability Failure to conduct a documented pre-rental walk-around inspection.
  • close
    Missing Maintenance Records Inability to show when hoses were last flushed or proportioners serviced.
