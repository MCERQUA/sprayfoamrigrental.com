## Pillar 1: Rent vs. Buy — Is Rig Rental Right for You?
- **Covers:** capital vs. operating cost, utilization thresholds, business-stage fit, testing a
  new service line, seasonal/backup use cases
- **Clusters into:** Post 4 (backup/overflow/new-service-line), Post 3 (pricing explainer,
  shared)
- **Internal links out:** Equipment Types, What to Check, Get Matched
- **Why it's the flagship:** captures the largest real search volume in the niche ("spray foam
  rig," "spray foam rig for sale," "used spray foam rig for sale" — 1,000/mo each) with content
  no competitor can neutrally publish.

## Pillar 2: What Equipment Is Typically Available to Rent
- **Covers:** proportioner, spray gun, heated hose, generator/power, open vs. closed cell
  material basics, what's bundled vs. separate
- **Clusters into:** Post 1 (component deep-dive)
- **Internal links out:** Rent vs. Buy, What to Check, Get Matched

## Pillar 3: What to Check Before You Rent a Rig
- **Covers:** insurance/liability, delivery/logistics, rental terms, material compatibility,
  support/training availability, pricing-structure comparison, red flags
- **Clusters into:** Post 2 (Home Depot FAQ — corrects confusion, funnels into evaluation
  mindset), Post 3 (pricing explainer, shared)
- **Internal links out:** Rent vs. Buy, Equipment Types, Get Matched
- **Why it matters most for conversion:** the natural bridge point where "evaluating a rental
  provider" becomes "maybe I should just get matched with an insured pro instead" — strongest
  CTA placement on the site.

## Supporting/conversion pages
- **Get Matched** — sole hard-conversion destination; every pillar and post links here.
- **Home** — orientation hub, light version of all 3 pillars, routes traffic down.
- **About** — E-E-A-T/trust anchor, discloses CCA's real business and this site's honest scope.
- **Contact** — utility, routes rental-transaction questions away (not CCA's business) and
  contractor-matching questions to Get Matched.

## Internal linking rules
1. Every blog post links to at least 1 pillar page + Get Matched.
2. Every pillar page links to the other 2 pillars + Get Matched + at least 1 relevant post.
3. Home links to all 3 pillars + Get Matched + latest blog posts.
4. Get Matched is the only page with zero outbound content links other than About/Contact (it's
   the funnel terminus).
5. No orphan pages — Privacy/Terms link from footer only, not part of the topical cluster.
