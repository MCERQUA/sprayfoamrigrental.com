# Content Brief: About

## Purpose
Establish who runs the site and why, transparently — important for trust given the honest-guide
positioning and to satisfy E-E-A-T for a site that discusses cost/business decisions.

## Target keywords
Secondary/branded only: sprayfoamrigrental.com, Contractors Choice Agency spray foam

## Page sections
1. **Who operates this site** — clearly state Contractors Choice Agency (CCA), an insurance
   agency that works with spray foam and construction trades, built this resource to help
   contractors and property owners understand rig rental as part of researching their options.
2. **Why an insurance agency publishes equipment-rental content** — honest explanation: CCA
   works daily with spray foam contractors on insurance and sees how often equipment decisions
   (rent, buy, or hire out) intersect with coverage questions; this site shares that practical
   knowledge.
3. **What this site is not** — a direct, short disclosure: not a rental marketplace, doesn't
   own or broker rig rentals; an educational resource only.
4. **CCA's real service** — brief description of the Get Matched / insured-contractor-network
   business, with a link.
5. **Contact info** — canonical CCA HQ (12220 E Riggs Rd, Chandler, AZ 85249), phone, email.

350-500 words

Direct, transparent — this page is partly a trust/honesty mechanism, not just boilerplate.
