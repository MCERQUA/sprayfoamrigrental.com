# Content Brief: Home

## Purpose
Establish sprayfoamrigrental.com as an honest, practical educational resource on spray foam rig
equipment rental — orient both audiences (contractors weighing rent vs. buy, curious property
owners) fast, and route toward CCA's real Get Matched service without claiming a rental
marketplace exists here.

## Target keywords
Primary: spray foam rig rental, spray foam equipment rental, spray foam rig
Secondary: spray foam machine rental, spray foam rental equipment, rent vs buy spray foam
equipment

## Search intent
Mixed navigational/informational — visitors landing here are early in research, not ready to
transact.

## Page sections
1. **Hero** — plain-spoken headline establishing the site as a guide, not a marketplace (e.g.
   "Renting a Spray Foam Rig? Here's What Contractors Need to Know" — working title, design
   phase to refine). Sub-headline clarifies: educational resource, not a booking platform.
2. **The three core questions** — teaser cards linking to the three pillar pages: Rent vs. Buy /
   What Equipment Is Available / What to Check Before You Rent.
3. **Why rig rental is a real decision** — 2-3 short paragraphs: seasonal capacity, backup
   equipment, testing a new service line, avoiding large capital outlay. Cite the real
   competitive landscape generically ("a genuine, competitive market of rental providers exists
   nationwide") without naming/endorsing specific competitors.
4. **Equipment-type primer (condensed)** — proportioner, spray gun, heated hose, generator —
   one line each, links to the full equipment page.
5. **Trust/honesty note** — brief, plain statement that this is CCA's educational resource, not
   a rental booking service, and what CCA actually does (insured-contractor matching).
6. **Primary CTA block** — "Get Matched with a Tech-Forward, Insured Spray Foam Contractor" —
   framed as: whether you rent, buy, or hire out the work, if you need an insured spray foam
   contractor, CCA can match you with one.
7. **Blog teaser** — 2-3 latest post cards.

## Internal links
→ Rent vs. Buy, Equipment Types, What to Check Before You Rent, Get Matched, Blog

## Word count target
600-900 words (hub page, not a deep-dive)

## Tone notes
Practical, cost-conscious, plain-spoken. No marketplace/booking language ("browse rigs,"
"reserve now," "available in your area") anywhere on this page.
