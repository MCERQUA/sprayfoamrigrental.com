# Content Brief: What to Check Before You Rent a Rig

## Purpose
The single clearest whitespace identified in competitor research — a genuinely neutral
evaluation checklist no rental operator can credibly publish about their own business. Positions
sprayfoamrigrental.com as the trustworthy, non-selling voice in the space.

## Target keywords
Primary: spray foam equipment rental company reviews, how to choose a spray foam equipment
rental company
Secondary: spray foam rig rental insurance requirements, spray foam equipment rental delivery,
spray foam equipment rental contract, spray foam equipment rental near me

## Search intent
Commercial investigation — late-stage research, close to choosing a provider (whichever provider
that ends up being; this page doesn't recommend one).

## Page sections
1. **Framing** — a short, honest note: this is a checklist for evaluating ANY rental provider
   you're considering; sprayfoamrigrental.com doesn't rent equipment itself.
2. **Insurance & liability** — what to confirm the provider carries, what you may be asked to
   show as the renter (real pattern found: San Antonio's process requires renter insurance
   confirmation), and why this matters for your own business's liability exposure.
3. **Delivery & logistics** — service-area coverage, delivery fees, site-access requirements
   (power, space, vehicle access).
4. **Rental terms** — minimum rental period, deposit/damage policy, cancellation/rescheduling
   terms, what happens if equipment fails mid-job.
5. **Material compatibility** — confirming the rig is set up (and calibrated) for the specific
   material/chemistry you plan to use.
6. **Support & training** — whether on-site technician support or training is included (varies
   widely across the market — name the range generically).
7. **Pricing structure** — ask providers to clarify whether they price per-unit, by tier, or as
   a flat time-block (reference the pattern found in the competitive landscape) so quotes can
   actually be compared apples-to-apples.
8. **Red flags checklist** — quick bulleted list (no insurance proof offered, no clear rental
   agreement, no service-area/delivery clarity, vague damage-liability terms).
9. **CTA** — "If evaluating rental providers feels like more risk than you want to take on,
   get matched with a tech-forward, insured spray foam contractor instead" — the strongest,
   most natural CTA placement on the site.
