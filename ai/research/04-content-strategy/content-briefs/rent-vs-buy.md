# Content Brief: Rent vs. Buy — Is Rig Rental Right for You?

## Purpose
The site's flagship page. Capture the significant buy-intent search volume ("spray foam rig for
sale," "used spray foam rig" — 1,000/mo each) with a genuinely neutral decision framework no
competitor can credibly publish (see `03-competitor-analysis/gaps-opportunities.md` #3).

## Target keywords
Primary: rent vs buy spray foam equipment, spray foam rig
Secondary: should i rent or buy spray foam equipment, spray foam equipment rental vs purchase,
is it cheaper to rent or buy a spray foam rig, spray foam rig cost, spray foam startup cost

## Search intent
Commercial/comparison — highest-value page on the site for conversion.

## Page sections
1. **Framing** — this is a genuinely situational decision; there's no universally correct
   answer, and this page walks through the real variables.
2. **When renting typically makes sense** — testing a new market/service line before a capital
   commitment, seasonal or one-off overflow capacity, backup equipment while a primary rig is
   serviced, a new business not yet ready for a $60K-$120K+ purchase (cite the competitor-
   observed range generically, sourced to industry norms, not to one named competitor).
3. **When buying typically makes sense** — high, steady utilization; full control over
   maintenance/scheduling; long-term cost per job favors ownership past a break-even utilization
   threshold; brand/equipment consistency across a growing crew.
4. **The real cost variables to weigh** — purchase price range, financing costs, maintenance/
   downtime risk, storage/transport, rental day-rate vs. per-unit vs. flat-block pricing models
   (reference the pattern-analysis findings generically), delivery fees, insurance requirements
   either way.
5. **A simple utilization gut-check** — rough framework (e.g., "if you'd use a rig fewer than X
   days a month, renting is usually more efficient than owning" — present as general industry
   reasoning, not a fabricated precise formula/calculator claiming CCA data).
6. **Either way, insurance matters** — bridge paragraph: renting or buying, operating spray foam
   equipment as a business means carrying the right coverage. Transition into CTA.
7. **CTA** — Get Matched with an insured, tech-forward spray foam contractor (for those who
   decide the smarter move is hiring a pro instead of renting/buying at all — a legitimate third
   option this page should acknowledge).
