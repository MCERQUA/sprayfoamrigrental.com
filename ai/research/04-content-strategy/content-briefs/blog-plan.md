# Content Brief: Blog Plan (4 launch posts)

Blog supports the pillar pages, targets long-tail/FAQ keywords, and gives ongoing internal-
linking and freshness signal. All 4 chosen from real search demand found in keyword research
and PAA data.

## Post 1: "Spray Foam Proportioner, Gun, and Heated Hose: What Each Part of a Rig Actually Does"
- **Keywords:** spray foam proportioner rental, spray foam gun rental, heated hose rental,
  spray foam rig equipment
- **Intent:** informational, top-of-funnel equipment education
- **Angle:** deep-dive companion to the Equipment Types pillar page — one component at a time,
  written for someone who's heard these terms but doesn't know what they mean yet.
- **Word count:** 1,000-1,300

## Post 2: "Does Home Depot Rent Spray Foam Equipment? (And Where People Actually Get It)"
- **Keywords:** spray foam machine rental home depot, spray foam rig rental home depot, where
  can I rent a spray foam insulation machine
- **Intent:** informational, corrects a real recurring confusion found directly in related-
  search data
- **Angle:** honest, short, direct answer (no — general rental chains don't stock specialized
  plural-component spray foam rigs) plus a factual explanation of why (calibration, heated-hose
  requirements, material handling), then point toward the site's own Rent vs. Buy / What to
  Check pages for next steps.
- **Word count:** 600-900

## Post 3: "How Much Does It Cost to Rent Spray Foam Equipment? Understanding the Pricing Range"
- **Keywords:** spray foam equipment rental cost, spray foam rig rental cost, spray foam
  proportioner rental cost, spray foam rental rates
- **Intent:** commercial investigation
- **Angle:** explain the real reasons rental pricing varies so widely across the market (per-
  unit vs. tiered vs. flat time-block vs. percent-savings framing — sourced from the pattern
  analysis, described generically, no fabricated CCA-specific numbers), plus the cost variables
  that actually drive a quote up or down (rig capacity, rental duration, delivery distance,
  included support/training, material vs. equipment-only).
- **Word count:** 1,000-1,300

## Post 4: "Backup Rig, Overflow Capacity, or New Service Line? 3 Real Reasons Contractors Rent Instead of Buy"
- **Keywords:** backup spray foam rig, temporary spray foam equipment, seasonal spray foam
  equipment rental, short term spray foam equipment rental
- **Intent:** informational/commercial, use-case-driven
- **Angle:** three short case-pattern sections (not fabricated case studies, but honestly framed
  as "common situations contractors describe"): (1) a primary rig is down for service/repair,
  (2) seasonal demand spike beyond current rig capacity, (3) testing whether adding spray foam
  as a new service line is worth a capital purchase. Each section links back to Rent vs. Buy.
- **Word count:** 900-1,200

## Cadence & internal linking
All 4 posts link to at least one pillar page (Rent vs. Buy, Equipment Types, or What to Check)
and to Get Matched as a secondary CTA. Recommend publishing in the order listed — Post 1 and 2
are easiest/fastest wins, Posts 3 and 4 build toward conversion-adjacent topics.
