# Content Strategy Overview — sprayfoamrigrental.com

## Strategy summary
An educational buyer's-guide, not a rental marketplace. Three pillar pages carry the SEO and
conversion weight (Rent vs. Buy, Equipment Types, What to Check Before You Rent), supported by
4 launch blog posts and funneling every page toward CCA's real Get Matched service. See
individual briefs in `content-briefs/` for page-level detail.

## Why this content set, not a bigger one
The niche's real search volume is modest (see keyword-research). Rather than padding with thin
pages chasing zero-volume long-tails, the strategy concentrates depth into 3 pillar pages that
can each rank for a cluster of related terms and absorb the long-tail via on-page FAQ/schema,
plus 4 blog posts chosen specifically because each maps to a real, evidenced search gap
(competitor pattern gaps + PAA/related-search data) rather than generic industry content.

## Cross-cutting rules
- Never claim CCA operates a rig-rental marketplace, matching service, or brokers rental
  transactions.
- Never invent specific rental prices, provider names as partners, or fabricated case studies —
  use "common situations contractors describe" framing, not manufactured testimonials.
- Every page's CTA funnels to Get Matched, framed as a legitimate third option (vs. rent, vs.
  buy) — hiring an insured pro instead — never as "click here to rent a rig."
- Tone: practical, cost-conscious, plain-spoken throughout; no marketplace/booking language.
