## Search intent notes

- **Commercial/comparison intent** dominates: "rent vs buy," "rental cost," "rental company
  reviews," "what to check before you rent" — these are the pages that should carry the
  strongest Get Matched CTA placement.
- **Informational intent**: "what equipment is available," "how spray foam rigs work,"
  "proportioner vs. gun vs. heated hose" — educational, links back to commercial pages and to
  Get Matched as a secondary CTA.
- **Transactional/navigational intent** ("spray foam rig for sale," "used spray foam rig")
  belongs to the buy-side, NOT this rental-education site — do not chase it directly with a
  sales page (CCA doesn't sell rigs), but the Rent vs. Buy page should absolutely target the
  searcher who typed a buy-intent query and is still deciding.
