# Competitor Pattern Analysis — Spray Foam Rig Rental Space

Synthesized from 7 site reviews (see `site-reviews/`). All 7 are genuine operating rental
businesses — this confirms rig rental is a real, competed-for niche, not a fabricated market.

## Recurring business models observed

1. **Rent-to-DIY** (sprayfoamrentals.com, sprayfoamrentalsnyc.com) — rig + material delivered,
   customer installs with technician guidance. Targets homeowners and non-specialist
   contractors as much as SPF pros.
2. **Rent-to-scale / try-before-you-buy** (sprayalliance.com) — positions rental as a low-risk
   path to eventually owning a specific rig model; blurs into equipment sales.
3. **Rent-to-launch-a-business** (sanantoniosprayfoamrentals.com, allfoamtech.com) — targets
   people entering the spray foam trade who can't yet justify a $60K–$120K rig purchase.
4. **Rent-for-overflow/backup capacity** (chicagolandsprayfoam.com, masterpkg.com) — targets
   established contractors needing short-term extra capacity for a specific job or season.

sprayfoamrigrental.com's educational angle sits *across* all four models — it's the only entrant
that doesn't pick one business model and sell it, because it isn't selling rental transactions at
all. That's a structural advantage for neutral, comparison-style content.

## Pricing models seen (name generically, never invent CCA-specific numbers)

- **Per-unit rate** (San Antonio: $750/unit proportioner, $350/unit vacuum trailer)
- **Tiered rig models priced as purchases-with-a-rental-option** (Spray Alliance: $43,810–
  $55,513)
- **Range-priced by equipment type** (NYC: $350–$1,300 across the catalog)
- **Flat time-block rate** (Chicagoland: $1,150 flat per 9-hour block)
- **Percent-savings-vs-contractor framing, no absolute numbers** (Master Pack, sprayfoamrentals.com:
  20–40% savings claims without base pricing)

Takeaway: pricing presentation is inconsistent industry-wide. This is itself a content
opportunity — "why rental pricing varies so much" is a legitimate, honest educational section.

## Common trust signals

- Graco authorized-distributor status (3 of 7 explicitly)
- Insurance claims ("100% owned, insured, and maintained" — San Antonio only; most others are
  silent on insurance)
- On-site training/technician support (sprayfoamrentals.com, NYC, Master Pack)
- Testimonials (sprayfoamrentals.com, allfoamtech.com)
- Certifications (Master Pack's Class 1 Fire Retardant material cert; allfoamtech.com's Top 20
  Distributor recognition)
- Defined service area / physical address (all 7)

## Common content gaps across the competitor set

- **No competitor publishes a genuine "what to check before you rent from any provider"
  buyer-education page** — every site's content serves its own booking funnel, not neutral
  evaluation criteria. This is the single clearest whitespace for sprayfoamrigrental.com.
- **No competitor explains equipment TYPES (proportioner vs. gun vs. heated hose vs. generator)
  independent of trying to rent you one** — content exists only as product-catalog copy.
- **Insurance requirements for renters are mentioned by only one competitor** (San Antonio) and
  buried in a 6-step process list, not explained.
- **None frame rent-vs-buy as a genuinely neutral decision** — Spray Alliance's "try before you
  buy" is the closest, but it's still selling toward a purchase of their specific rig line.
- **None connect a rental/buy decision to insurance for the contracting business itself** — a
  natural, honest bridge to CCA's real Get Matched / insured-contractor-network service, since
  every renter or buyer of this equipment is also a business that needs liability/equipment
  coverage.
