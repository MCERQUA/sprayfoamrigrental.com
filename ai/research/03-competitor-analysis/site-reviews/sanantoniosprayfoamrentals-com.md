# Competitor Review: sanantoniosprayfoamrentals.com

Reviewed for content/structure patterns only — not scraped verbatim, not implied as a CCA
partner or affiliate.

## Structure
- Home (hero), Contact, FAQ, shopping-cart/store functionality with featured products.

## Business model / tagline
- "You bring the job. We bring the rig." Positions itself as a barrier-removal solution for
  contractors entering the spray foam trade, contrasting $60K–$120K ownership cost vs. rental.

## Equipment / pricing
- Graco R2E30 and R3E30 proportioners at $750/unit (rental unit rate), Insulation Vacuum with
  22' flatbed trailer at $350/unit — genuinely itemized, transparent per-unit pricing, unusual
  in this competitor set.

## Target audiences named
- Homeowners without experience, experienced contractors, builders/developers, investors/PMs.

## Trust signals
- "100% owned, insured, and maintained" claim
- Maintenance/inspection protocol messaging
- Six-step rental process outlined (contact → insurance requirement → security deposit → ... →
  return)

## CTAs / lead capture
- "Book Now" SMS number, "Add to Cart" per item, contact form, dedicated email

## Takeaway for sprayfoamrigrental.com
The clearest example of transparent, itemized rental pricing in the set — useful pattern to
reference generically ("rental pricing models vary — some providers quote per-unit rates, others
flat day rates") in the cost-factors content, without inventing sprayfoamrigrental-specific
numbers. Their explicit insurance-requirement step in the rental process is a legitimate "what
to check before you rent" content point (renters should expect to show or obtain insurance).
