# Competitor Review: sprayalliance.com/rental-rigs (Spray Alliance Corp — Rig Rental Program)

Reviewed for content/structure patterns only — not scraped verbatim, not implied as a CCA
partner or affiliate.

## Structure
- Hero: "Need a Rig in a Hurry?" — three tiered equipment models displayed prominently, program
  benefits section, technology/upgrade promotion, footer with quick links.

## Equipment / pricing tiers
| Model | Positioning | Starting price |
|---|---|---|
| R3 E20 7KW | Backup/small jobs | $43,810 |
| R3 E20 Elite w/E1 | Higher output/precision | $50,269 |
| R3 E30 Elite 15KW | Heavy daily use | $55,513 |

All include heated hoses, guns, proportioners, electric/air pumps. Note: these are *purchase*
prices for rig models available under a rental/test-drive program, not daily rental rates —
a distinct model from the day-rate competitors below.

## Content themes
- Solving capacity gaps for contractors, equipment access without major capital outlay,
  "try before you buy" quality assurance, flexibility for short vs. long-term needs.

## Trust signals
- "Risk Free" / "Try Before You Buy" program framing
- Authorized Graco distributor
- Complex-installation experience (high-rises) referenced
- Service & repair center in footer

## CTAs / lead capture
- "Contact Us" + "Get Started" per model, phone/email/address, WhatsApp option, custom-job
  inquiry path

## Takeaway for sprayfoamrigrental.com
The "try before you buy" framing is the single most relevant competitor pattern for this site's
honest angle — it directly supports a legitimate Rent vs. Buy content argument (renting/testing
before committing to a large capital purchase) without requiring sprayfoamrigrental.com to claim
it operates the program itself.
