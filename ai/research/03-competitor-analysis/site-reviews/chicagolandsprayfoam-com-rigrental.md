## Takeaway for sprayfoamrigrental.com
The flat-rate, time-blocked pricing model (X hours for a flat fee, delivered) is a distinct
pricing pattern from the per-unit or tiered-purchase models seen elsewhere in this set — worth
naming generically in the cost-factors content ("some providers charge a flat rate per rental
block, others price per unit or by tier") since it's a real pattern a contractor will encounter
when shopping providers. (Note: harboursprayfoam.com/rental was also surfaced during this
research with a similar time/flat-rate model — not one of the seven required competitors, so not
separately reviewed, but confirms the flat-rate pattern is common, not a one-off.)
