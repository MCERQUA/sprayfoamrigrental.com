# Competitor Review: sprayfoamrentals.com (U.S. Spray Foam Rentals)

Reviewed for content/structure patterns only — not scraped verbatim, not implied as a CCA
partner or affiliate.

## Structure
- Single-page-heavy layout: Home, About Us, FAQ, Equipment/Materials, Projects, Videos, Blog,
  Contact/Quote
- Prominent CTA buttons throughout, social integration (Facebook, Instagram)

## Business model
- Nationwide mobile equipment rental: customer rents rig + material and installs it themselves
  with technician guidance on-site — positioned as a DIY-adjacent alternative to hiring a
  contractor.

## Equipment
- Professional-grade rigs delivered to the job site, both closed-cell and open-cell material,
  on-site technician training/supervision, material-quantity consultation.

## Trust signals
- 10+ testimonials from multiple states
- "25–40% less than contractor" cost-savings claim
- "Highest quality" U.S.-manufactured material claim
- No visible insurance, warranty, or formal certification claims

## CTAs / lead capture
- "Get a Quote" buttons (multiple placements), direct phone number, contact/quote form

## Takeaway for sprayfoamrigrental.com
This is the clearest example in the set of the "we operate a rental marketplace" claim style —
exactly the framing this project is deliberately NOT copying. Useful pattern to borrow honestly:
technician-guidance / on-site support messaging reduces DIY-risk anxiety, which is a legitimate
educational point to make generically (without claiming CCA offers it).
