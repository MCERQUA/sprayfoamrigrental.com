# Competitor Review: allfoamtech.com

Reviewed for content/structure patterns only — not scraped verbatim, not implied as a CCA
partner or affiliate.

## Structure
- Nav: Home, About, Services, Contact, Blog, Shop; dropdown covers Foam Parts, Tech Support,
  Spray Foam Rigs, Start a Business, Training & Application, Knowledge Center. English/Spanish
  support.

## Services
- Rig sales/rental, tech support, training and application services, business-startup
  consultation for new contractors, parts/supplies retail.

## Trust signals
- Authorized Graco distributor, "Top 20 Distributor" (FPE) recognition, partner logos
  (Bullard, PMC), team credentials (installation/contracting/distribution backgrounds), one
  named customer testimonial.

## Pricing
- None on homepage; directs to Shop section.

## CTAs
- Phone (prominent), email, two physical addresses (FL and CT), contact form.

## Takeaway for sprayfoamrigrental.com
The "Start a Business" content pillar is the most directly relevant pattern: it validates that
"should I rent or buy to start/scale a spray foam business" is a real, competitively-covered
content angle — reinforces this site's Rent vs. Buy page as core, not a stretch topic.
