# Competitor Review: sprayfoamrentalsnyc.com (Spray Foam Rentals NYC)

Reviewed for content/structure patterns only — not scraped verbatim, not implied as a CCA
partner or affiliate.

## Structure
- Nav: Home, Rental Equipment, About Us, Blog, Recent Projects, Ordering Info, Contact Us.
  Hero: "NYC's Premier Spray Foam Rental Solutions." Product catalog + contact forms
  distributed throughout.

## Content themes
- Energy-efficiency/cost-savings framing, benefits education (thermal performance, moisture
  control, soundproofing), open-cell vs. closed-cell explainer content, rent-vs-buy advantages.

## Equipment
- Full Graco Reactor line (E30, E20, E10, 3 Elite E-30, Reactor 2 E-30), Graco ToughTek
  fireproofing units, insulation blowing machines, PC-series open/closed-cell materials,
  heated hoses/accessories.

## Pricing
- Equipment rental prices listed in a $350–$1,300 range, "20–30% savings vs. hiring a
  contractor" claim. Material pricing listed as "$0.00" (i.e., quote-on-request).

## Trust signals
- Physical address (Staten Island, NY), defined service area (NYC/NJ/CT), partner mentions with
  other regional spray foam businesses, directory badges, social links.

## CTAs / lead capture
- Three phone numbers listed, contact form, "Add to Cart," "Rent me Now!" button, "Schedule
  Today!" CTA.

## Takeaway for sprayfoamrigrental.com
Strong example of educational-content-plus-rental-catalog hybrid — the open-cell vs. closed-cell
explainer and thermal/moisture/soundproofing benefit framing is exactly the kind of neutral,
factual equipment education this site should include on the "What Equipment Is Typically
Available to Rent" page, without the product-catalog/cart mechanics.
