# Conversion Patterns — Adapted for an Educational Guide (Not a Marketplace)

Source: CTA/trust-signal/funnel patterns observed across the 7 competitor reviews (see
`03-competitor-analysis/`), adapted honestly for sprayfoamrigrental.com's non-transactional
positioning funneling to CCA's real Get Matched service.

## CTA hierarchy for this site

1. **Primary (every page):** "Get Matched with a Tech-Forward, Insured Spray Foam Contractor" —
   consistent label, links to /get-matched.
2. **Secondary (pillar pages only):** cross-links to the other 2 pillar pages ("Not sure if
   renting is right for you? See what to check before you rent →").
3. **Tertiary (footer, all pages):** phone + email, framed as "questions about getting matched
   with an insured contractor."

## Trust-signal plan (honest substitutes for what competitors use)

- **Transparency disclosure** (About page + a brief home-page note): plainly states this is an
  educational resource from an insurance agency, not a rental service — an unusual trust signal
  (most competitors never volunteer limitations) that differentiates the site as credible.
- **Sourced, generic industry data** instead of fabricated stats — e.g., citing the real range of
  pricing models found in the competitive landscape (per-unit, tiered, flat-block) rather than
  claiming a specific number as fact.
- **Canonical, verifiable CCA contact info** on every page (no fabricated support channels, no
  fake live-chat, no fake "24/7 support" claims).
- **No urgency/scarcity manipulation** ("only 2 rigs left," "book now before they're gone") —
  none of that applies to an educational site and using it would be actively dishonest here.

## Funnel summary

```
Organic search (informational/comparison intent)
        |
        v
Pillar page or blog post (answers the real question honestly)
        |
        v
Soft CTA: "If you'd rather hire a pro than rent or buy equipment yourself..."
        |
        v
Get Matched page (CCA's real service, clearly described)
        |
        v
Contact form / phone / email → CCA
```

No step in this funnel implies a rental transaction, booking, or marketplace interaction at any
point.
