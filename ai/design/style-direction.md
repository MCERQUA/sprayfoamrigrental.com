# Style Direction — sprayfoamrigrental.com

## Palette (chosen; genuinely distinct from all 5 prior fleet builds)
Prior builds all paired a warm primary with a GREEN secondary (sage, verdigris, marsh-green,
olive-moss, pine-green) — 5 for 5. This build breaks that pattern: no green anywhere.

- Primary: mahogany-rust `hsl(16 60% 32%)` (~#82401F) — CTAs, links, primary accents
- Accent: amber-honey `hsl(38 70% 47%)` (~#C88A26) — secondary CTAs, footer highlights, badges
- Base: warm ivory-parchment `hsl(36 42% 95%)` (~#F7F0E4)
- Dark contrast band: espresso-charcoal `hsl(20 30% 10%)` (~#221812)
- Zero purple/pink/blue/emoji — verified against globals.css before build.

## Typography
Bitter (heading, 600/700/800) + Source Sans 3 (body) — not used on any prior fleet build this
batch. Bitter's sturdy slab-serif character fits the "practical, cost-conscious, plain-spoken"
tone better than a display sans; Source Sans 3 keeps body copy legible and unpretentious.

## Motif
"Loaned equipment / temporary use" — a trailer-hitch/rig-in-transit idea rather than repeating
any prior motif (blueprint-scan, measurement-grid, gauge-dial, coiled-hose-line). Section
band-texture uses a subtle repeating linked-oval pattern echoing hitch couplings. Navbar/Footer/
StickyCTA icon: `Truck` (Lucide) — distinct from `Gauge` used on sprayfoamrigtech.com.

## Tone
Practical, cost-conscious, plain-spoken. A contractor weighing rent vs. buy — never marketplace/
booking language ("browse rigs," "reserve now," "available in your area"). Every page carries the
honest-guide framing established in the business profile: this is CCA's educational resource, not
a rental marketplace.

## Section rhythm
base (ivory) → tan/sand (secondary, textured) → charcoal (dark contrast) → accent-wash (amber
15%) → card — rotated per the standing no-monotone-page rule, at least one charcoal band per page.
