# Design Brief — sprayfoamrigrental.com

## What makes this site distinct from the other 5 fleet builds
1. **First build with no green secondary accent** — mahogany-rust + amber-honey only. Every
   prior build paired warm primary + green secondary; this one deliberately breaks that pattern.
2. **First use of Bitter + Source Sans 3** — a sturdier slab-serif heading face fits the
   practical/cost-conscious tone better than the condensed-industrial or geometric-sans pairings
   used elsewhere.
3. **First "loaned equipment" motif** — Truck icon, hitch-coupling band texture — distinct from
   the gauge/blueprint/measurement motifs used on prior builds.
4. **Content angle is the sharpest honesty-differentiator in the batch**: the "What to Check
   Before You Rent" pillar page is content literally no real competitor can credibly publish
   (it would undercut their own booking funnel) — verified via 7 real competitor site reviews.

## What beats competitors (from gaps-opportunities.md)
- A genuinely neutral rent-vs-buy decision framework (no competitor sells both sides of that
  decision credibly).
- Equipment education with zero brand/model endorsement — informational, not a catalog.
- Insurance-as-bridge trust signal, elevated rather than buried (only 1 of 7 competitors even
  mentions renter insurance requirements).

## Non-negotiables carried from the business profile
- Never claim CCA operates a live rental-matching/brokering marketplace.
- Every CTA reads "Get Matched," never "quote," "book," or "reserve."
- No fabricated testimonials, no percent-savings stats, no urgency/scarcity language.
