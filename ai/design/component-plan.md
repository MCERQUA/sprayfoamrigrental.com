# Component Plan — sprayfoamrigrental.com

Reused the proven shared-component set from prior fleet builds (Section, Button, TrustBadge,
TechCard, FAQAccordion, CaveatCallout, Navbar, Footer, StickyCTA, LeadForm/ContactForm/AEOFields)
— rebranded (Truck icon, mahogany/amber palette, no pine-green) rather than redesigned from
scratch, since the underlying UX patterns (sticky mobile call/get-matched bar, 4-field lead form,
band-rhythm sections) are already validated across 5 builds.

## Homepage flow (per home.md content brief + Local Service CRO rhythm)
1. Hero (full-bleed image, ≥50% scrim, plain-spoken headline + honest-guide sub-headline)
2. Trust/definition band (tan) — what this site is / isn't
3. Three-pillar teaser grid (TechCard x3 — Rent vs. Buy / Equipment / What to Check)
4. "Why rig rental is a real decision" band (base) — seasonal, backup, new-service-line, capital
5. Equipment primer condensed (accent-wash) — proportioner/gun/hose/generator one-liners
6. Honesty/trust note (CaveatCallout-style, base) — CCA's real service, not a rental booking site
7. Primary CTA band (charcoal) — Get Matched
8. Blog teaser (card, 3 posts)
9. Footer

## Pillar pages (Rent vs. Buy, Equipment Types, What to Check)
Standard: hero band → body content bands (rotating base/tan/accent-wash) → FAQAccordion (schema)
→ CaveatCallout where a limitation/honesty note fits naturally → CTA band (charcoal) → cross-links
to other 2 pillars.

## Get Matched
Short, conversion-focused: hero → LeadForm (card) → how-it-works 3-step → FAQ (short) → footer.
No pillar-page depth — per content brief, 500-800 words only.
