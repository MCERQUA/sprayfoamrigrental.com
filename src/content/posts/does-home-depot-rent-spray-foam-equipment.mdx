## Why General Rental Chains Don't Carry Spray Foam Rigs

A spray foam rig isn't a single tool you hand over the counter — it's a calibrated system: a
proportioner that has to be set up correctly for the specific chemical formulation you're
using, a heated hose that needs to hold temperature reliably, and a spray gun that has to be
maintained between uses. General equipment rental businesses are built around tools that work
the same way for every renter — pressure washers, generators, tile saws. Spray foam equipment
doesn't fit that model. It needs calibration specific to the material, and a rental company
has to actually understand spray foam chemistry to hand it over safely and get it back working.

That's a specialized business, and it's why the companies that do rent spray foam rigs are
spray-foam-specific operators, not general tool rental counters.
