If you've gotten a few quotes from different spray foam equipment rental providers and the
numbers don't seem to line up, you're not imagining it. Pricing in this market varies more
than most rental categories — and understanding why makes it much easier to compare quotes
that actually mean the same thing.

## Why Rental Pricing Varies So Widely

Across the competitive landscape, providers price rig rentals a few different ways:

- **Per-unit pricing** — a flat rate for the proportioner, gun, and hose package as one bundle.
- **Tiered pricing** — rates that scale with proportioner output capacity or rig size.
- **Flat time-block pricing** — a set rate for a defined rental period (day, week, or month)
  regardless of usage within that window.

None of these approaches is inherently "the cheap one" or "the expensive one" — they just
structure the cost differently. A flat time-block rate might look higher upfront than a
per-unit day rate, but work out cheaper if your job runs long. This is exactly why comparing
two quotes without knowing which pricing model each one uses can be misleading.

## The Variables That Actually Move a Quote

Beyond the pricing model itself, a handful of real factors drive cost up or down:

- **Rig capacity** — higher-output proportioners generally cost more to rent than smaller units.
- **Rental duration** — longer rentals often (but not always) bring the effective daily rate down.
- **Delivery distance** — providers typically charge delivery fees scaled to distance from their
  location.
- **Included support or training** — some providers include on-site technician support or basic
  training in the rate; others charge separately or don't offer it at all.
- **Equipment-only vs. equipment-plus-material** — confirm whether a quote covers just the rig,
  or material as well.
